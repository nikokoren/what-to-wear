# German rewrites, round 23

You write German lines for a TRMNL e-ink weather screen ("What to Wear"). Voice: der Grantler, a grumpy, dry screen, standard German (no dialect). Write in German from scratch; never translate.

The owner just read every live German line. These slots lost lines to a veto, or have none at all. Write replacements: new ideas, not variations of what was voted out.

## What the owner keeps

- The screen's own grumpy stake (it's a screen, indoors, it only does forecasts and has opinions about that).
- Truths everyone recognizes, with bite. Sarcasm, not gentle observation.
- Clear on first read. Short.

## What the owner vetoes

- Wordplay on the forecast's words; „wie deine X“ jabs; predicting the reader's mishaps or behavior; explaining the joke.
- Anything the screen can't know; assumptions about how the reader lives (the tumble dryer „irgendwo im Haus“: not everyone rents a flat).
- Calling a broad time precise (*pünktlich* next to „heute Nachmittag“). Garment jokes that don't fit every layer (sleeves).
- Storms: no „geh rein“ lectures. The extra-cold layer is now just „der Schal“ (not Mütze und Handschuhe).
- Read `review/dont/de.md` for the full veto list with notes.
