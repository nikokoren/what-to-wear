# Critic, round 27 (de)

81 unit candidates + 9 drawing candidates in, 30 kept (keep counts from `cells.json`). All kept lines are copied unchanged; none needed a fix. Worst-case length (heute Nachmittag / kann die Winterjacke / sie, separator not counted) is at most 164 characters.

## units_10.arc_level (4)
- Verdeck: the convertible drivers who keep the top down later "mit Mütze, aber offen". A real scene with a stubborn reversal. (The idea of convertibles out in the warm was vetoed before, but this joke is the stubbornness, not the showing off. Watch it.)
- Touristen: "sogar die Touristen in kurzen Hosen recht": the bite is in the "sogar".
- Kellner: the overeager waiter who clears your plate as soon as you sit down. Instantly recognisable, and the warmth gets blamed.
- Kurzurlaub: "mehr Aus- und Einpacken als Erholung". Recognition humour that fits the off/on arc exactly.
- **Weakest:** Kurzurlaub (a second simile next to the Kellner one).

## units_10.p_wetter (3)
- Werbeprospekt over the head: "Endlich ist das Ding zu was gut." The punch lands on the junk mail.
- Gartenstühle: the weather's pettiness, timed right after someone wiped the chairs dry.
- Taschenschirm: the shower lasts as long as it takes to get a pocket umbrella open. Short and true.
- **Weakest:** Taschenschirm.

## units_10.shorts_early (4)
- Rotwein: legs only come out at room temperature. The best comparison this round.
- Statistik: "elf Tage ... Heute ist einer davon. Zur Hälfte." A made-up statistic that ends on a reversal.
- Männer in Shorts in der Bahn, breitbeinig, "Als wär's ein Angebot." Cruel to strangers, not to the reader.
- Kinder: the kids who wanted to leave in shorts were right, but you're not allowed to tell them.
- **Weakest:** Kinder (it shares the "X haben heute recht" form with the arc_level Touristen line, different slot and different joke).

## units_10.w_jacket (3)
- Lokalzeitung: "Die Stadt atmet auf." The punch is "drei Leute vor der Eisdiele". It mocks local news.
- häuten / Schlangen machen das eleganter: a good image with a dry put-down.
- Schlafshirt: the few who keep their jacket on are hiding a sleep shirt. A wince.
- **Weakest:** Schlafshirt (it brushes the live 11 line "was du drunter anhast", but here the joke is the people who keep the jacket on).

## units_10.w_scarf (3)
- nur bis zum Kinn geschminkt: specific, a little cruel, aimed at other people.
- Neun von zehn Schals ... the tenth was a present and never gets worn. A made-up statistic with a twist.
- Teillieferung: the warmth arrives as a partial delivery, enough for the neck while the rest is still on its way. Fits the slot (the coat stays).
- **Weakest:** Teillieferung.

## units_10.w_water (4)
- Hinweis der Wasserwerke: "Bitte nicht alle gleichzeitig trinken." A fake notice, short.
- Bahn: everyone sticks to the seats, "Beim Aufstehen hört man's." Pure wince.
- Büro-Ventilator turned so only its owner gets air. Petty office behaviour.
- Leitungswasser im Restaurant is "Verhandlungssache". Bites at the world.
- **Weakest:** Büro-Ventilator (fans are a familiar prop on this screen; the angle here is new).

## units_11.p_wet_again (3)
- Inkassobüro: the rain comes back on purpose, more attention than you get from anyone but debt collectors. Confident and cruel.
- Zwei Duschen ... "schon Körperpflege". A hygiene jab, short.
- "Zum Gutaussehen hätte bei dir auch kein Sommer gereicht." A looks jab with a good set-up.
- **Weakest:** Gutaussehen.

## units_11.p_wet_again_snow (3)
- Schuppenshampoo, "Das Vorher-Bild." The best 11 line this round.
- "Drinnen wär's jetzt richtig gemütlich. Nur halt nicht mit dir." Turns cosiness against the reader.
- "Der Schnee fällt heute zweimal auf dich rein. Die meisten Leute nur einmal." A pun on "reinfallen", not on the forecast's own words.
- **Weakest:** reinfallen (the pun needs half a beat).

## flavour_vf_10.fickle (3)
- „Typisch Aprilwetter“ ... "In jedem Monat außer April." Months tag kept (no April).
- Bahn wechselt das Gleis, "am Ende steht man wieder am alten": fits off/on/off exactly.
- Familienfeier: briefly warm, then "wieder alles unterkühlt". Sarcasm about the world.
- **Weakest:** Bahn/Gleis.
