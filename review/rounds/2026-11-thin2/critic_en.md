## Main cut patterns
1. **The same idea as a vetoed or live line in new words**: about 20 cuts, the biggest group (the snowman, "people who've met you", the group chat, coming back/"hint", the turtle-impression skeleton, defrost, "doing a lot for you").
2. **"Nobody notices you" cousins at 11**: about eight candidates; at most one survived per idea.
3. **Clever forms with a thin joke**: statistics (2%/98%, 90/10, 11%/80%), extra fake notices ("Attendance is mandatory", "for display only", "Lost: … No reward"). One notice per slot at most.
4. **Worn On moves**: the screen being smug about itself ("I draw…", "I always put the good half first", headstone, "rooting for it").
5. **Twins across slots**: sky maintenance (p_wetter / alone_10.wet), the sleeves (drawings / fickle), the umbrella drawn bigger (p_wetter_long / drawings), the reflection, "people come out" (p_drier / p_drier_snow), the ad comparisons (w_coat / p_drier_snow / wet). One of each kept, at most.
6. **Facts**: "40%", "hours ago", "past lunch", "just put out", the assumed window.
