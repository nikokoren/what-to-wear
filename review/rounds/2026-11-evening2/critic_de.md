## evening.jokes_10.rain (1)
- „Die Hunde ahnen noch nichts.“ Four words and a thriller-style hint, and it only works the night before: we know about the rain, the dogs don't. It's about the world, not a boast by the screen.
- Only 1: „Friseur“ is the starred hair idea and a cliché. „Bushaltestelle/Pfütze“ is a cliché that predicts a mishap and runs long. „Publikum“ is a forced personification. „Schirmständer … den Besitzer“ is a stock phrase and long. „Fahrradsättel tragen … Plastiktüte“ uses the same register-transfer trick as the vetoed „Pfützen: gute Tiefe, kräftiger Abgang“, and the bare noun can read as broken German.
- Flag: English live `alone_10.wet` has "Every dog that hates baths is about to get one anyway." Same premise (dogs vs. coming rain), but a different joke: there the rain is reframed as a bath, here it's the dogs not knowing yet. I kept it. The caller should confirm the cross-language rule allows it.

## evening.jokes_10.colder (1)
- „Ab dann haben wieder alle Omas recht.“ Short and confident, and it times the grandmas' eternal „zieh dir was an“ to the forecast's hour. It's about other people, so it fits On.
- Only 1: „Erst die gute Nachricht, dann die echte.“ repeats the live „Wär ja auch zu schön gewesen, wenn's gehalten hätte.“ and is unclear under „Morgen wird's ab 13 Uhr kälter.“, which gives no good news. „Wie die Dusche in jeder WG“ needs explaining and only fits the warm-start sentence. „Bahn … höhere Gewalt“ is the Bahn-weather-excuse cliché, and the snow version was vetoed.
- Flag: Oma is already used heavily (live `now_10.cold` Mütze, units `c_scarf` „Die Oma wär stolz auf mich“, `w_scarf`, `c_hoodie`). This line's joke (the grandmas proven right) is new, but the owner may find a fifth Oma line too many.

## evening.jokes_10.steady (1)
- „Endlich legt sich mal einer fest. Ausgerechnet das Wetter.“ A grumble about people who won't commit, with the weather (famously fickle) as the one exception. Natural spoken German.
- Only 1: „Mehr erwartet man ja auch nicht vom Leben.“ is flat and repeats the live „Wenn's nur immer so einfach wär“ and „alles andere schiefgehen“. „Ich häng übrigens nicht …“ is the screen insisting it's right (a banned On move), and „bleibt so“ is present tense for tomorrow. „Opa kam mit einem …“ only works by echoing the forecast's wording.
- Flag: English live `alone_10.fickle` "The weather can't commit." is the mirror image. Here the joke is about people and the weather is the exception, so I judge it a different joke.
