# Round 31: lines that told the same joke in both languages

The owner's rule: the two languages never translate each other. Where a
joke survived both critics in both languages, one side goes:

- "Fine. I suppose." / „Na gut. Wenn's sein muss.“: German kept (English
  has the owner's "Just what we needed." in rain, snow and colder).
- "If the forecast changes overnight, I'll redraw it. You, I can't fix." /
  „Das Wetter kann sich über Nacht noch ändern. Bei dir rechne ich nicht
  damit.“: German kept (its only new line at 11; English keeps two).
- "I may draw a few houses in the background. For morale." / „Ich mal dann
  halt ein bisschen Hintergrund…“: German kept (the owner's note on this
  direction was on the German sample).
