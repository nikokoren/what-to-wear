Outfit Panels Mock

Seven real days from station records, rendered through the actual markup with TRMNL's own styles. The day is shown as equal panels, left to right: what to wear now, then at each change. A day that doesn't change keeps one panel. Every image shows all four views on the chosen device and orientation. The drawings are still the placeholders.

  • Today only. Changes count until 21:00; nothing about tomorrow is drawn.
  • A change is an outfit step held for two hours, or rain or snow starting (one likely hour is enough) or stopping (two dry hours).
  • At most three panels. Changes less than two hours apart merge, outfit changes win over rain changes, and two panels that would look the same become one.
  • Now shows rain only while it falls. Rain later appears in the panel where it happens.
  • The last time reads "from 7 pm" ("ab 19 Uhr"): that outfit holds for the rest of the day.
  • Words. One panel: "works all day", plus the sarcastic line at On. Two or three panels: only the sarcastic line, and nothing at Off.

What to look at

  • The time sits under every drawing, in every view. Only the half view standing tall puts the panels one under the other.
  • The quarter view in portrait (240 by 400 on the OG) keeps the panels in a row: stacked, with the time under each, the drawings came out smaller.
  • On the half view lying wide, the words sit beside the panels so the drawings keep the full height.
  • The rainy coat is a placeholder copy of the dry coat, so a rainy coat panel shows no umbrella yet.
